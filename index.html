Who's behind it

A maker who refuses to pick a lane.

I'm a Computer Science student at De La Salle Lipa on the game-development track. My thesis, Circuit Breakers — a 2D cybersecurity roguelike built in Unity — won Best Thesis, Best Paper, and Best Presenter, where I served as lead author and ran the numerical balancing.

Beyond code I trained a convolutional neural network that classifies dermoscopic images at 90%+ accuracy, shipped front-end web apps, and spend my off-hours drawing, composing, and writing Canrael — a world I've been building for seven years. I'm equally at home in a balancing spreadsheet and a melancholic verse.
