Who's behind it

A maker who refuses to pick a lane.

I'm a fresh Computer Science graduate from De La Salle Lipa, where I took the game-development track. My thesis, Circuit Breakers (a 2D cybersecurity roguelike built in Unity), won Best Thesis, Best Paper, and Best Presenter. I led the paper and ran the numerical balancing.

I also trained a convolutional neural network that reads dermoscopic images at 90%+ accuracy, and I've shipped a few front-end web apps. Off the clock I draw, compose, and write Canrael, a world I've kept going for seven years.
