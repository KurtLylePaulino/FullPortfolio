CanraelA world, seven years deep

In a world where the gods have turned their gaze away, we are but whispers in the ruins of a forgotten age.

Canrael began as a Forgotten-Realms-inspired campaign setting and grew, over seven years, into something darker and wholly its own: a post-apocalyptic dark fantasy where the pantheons have gone silent and a catastrophe called the Convergence shattered the boundaries between worlds. What follows is a glimpse of its bones: its factions, its horrors, and the people who still light lamps against the dark. The full texts are linked below.

The dark and what holds it back

The Hunger Beneath

The Abyss

Not a place but a wound: a labyrinth of endless ruin that does not think, but remembers; does not plan, but waits. It births horrors, fractures time, and whispers to the desperate. It is ruled by things that were never meant to exist:

  • Morvath, the Starless Harbinger: North, the frozen void
  • Shyrr, the Maw: East, the gluttonous gloom
  • Vaezros, of Embers & Chains: South, the ashlands
  • Syltheris, Weaver of Fractured Light: West
The Last Light

The Lighthouses

Forged by the lost Kingdom of Ascalon, not from stone but from the bound souls of their greatest martyrs. Each burns with Aetherflame, a silver-blue light anathema to the dark. But the Evergems are failing, the Keepers are cursed to their lonely vigils, and one by one the lights go out.

The Shield of Humanity

The Empire

A dieselpunk leviathan of airships, steam-soldiers, and smog: self-proclaimed protector of mankind, ethnocentric in word yet paradoxically compassionate in deed. Its elite Praetorum and its legend, Wolmane, hold the line as industry rots the land beneath them.

Orders of the broken

Knights & Heretics

The Knights of the Broken Blade: a melancholic order who fight not for glory but for peace with their past, atoning in elegiac verse. Against them, the Abyssal Order: a machine-cult of rage led by a corrupted savior and his Thirteen Blades. A symphony of sorrows and a hymn of defiance.
